# FAQ

If your question isn't here, ask on the [HAMRS Discord](https://discord.gg/nngWMtXTqH).

## Subscriptions

### What needs a hamrs.app subscription?

[HAMRS Sync](/docs/sync/setting-up-sync), which keeps your logbooks, QSOs, and operator profiles the same on every device.

### Is that the same as a QRZ subscription?

No. [Uploading to your QRZ Logbook](/docs/integrations/qrz/uploading-logbooks) needs a paid QRZ.com XML subscription, which you buy from QRZ.

### Do I need a separate key for each device?

No. You paste the same API Key into every HAMRS app.

### Where do I manage my subscription?

Sign in to [hamrs.app](https://hamrs.app) and open **Manage your subscription**.

### What happens if my subscription lapses?

Sync pauses until you renew. Your data stays on your devices. See [Sync troubleshooting](/docs/sync/setting-up-sync#troubleshooting) for the message you'll see.

## Apps and versions

### Where do I download HAMRS?

From [hamrs.app](https://hamrs.app).

### Which version do I have?

The version number is at the bottom of the app window. On small screens it is in the navigation menu.

### How do updates arrive on desktop?

- **macOS and Windows:** HAMRS checks for updates when it starts and asks whether to download a new version.
- **Linux:** HAMRS tells you when a new version is out. Update through your system package manager, or download the latest AppImage from [hamrs.app](https://hamrs.app).

## Getting help

Ask on the [HAMRS Discord](https://discord.gg/nngWMtXTqH). Include your platform, your version number, and a screenshot of the error.

## Your data

### Where are my logs stored?

On the device you log on. If you use Sync, they are also stored in your hamrs.app account so your other devices can pull them down.

### How do I back up a logbook?

Choose **Export ADIF** from the logbook's menu and keep the `.adi` file somewhere safe.
